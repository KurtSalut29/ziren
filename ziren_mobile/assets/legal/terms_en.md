# Terms of Use

**Version 1.1 — effective 24 September 2026**

By using Ziren you agree to these terms. Please read them before you create an
account.

## 1. What Ziren is

Ziren lets you report an emergency in Biliran to the Bureau of Fire Protection,
the Philippine National Police, or your Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Office, and lets those agencies dispatch a response.

## 2. What Ziren is not

**Ziren does not replace the emergency hotlines.** If this app fails, your
phone has no signal, or your battery is dead, call 911 or your local emergency
number. Do not rely on this app as your only way to reach help.

A report is delivered over your phone's internet (mobile data or Wi-Fi)
connection. This is not guaranteed. Coverage in parts of Biliran is poor, and
if you have no internet connection at all, a report cannot be sent.

**Submitting a report does not guarantee a response.** Agencies decide how to
prioritise and allocate their crews. Ziren carries your report and shows the
dispatcher what it knows; it does not control who is sent, or when.

## 3. Your responsibilities

**Report honestly.** Tell us what you actually saw or experienced. If you are
unsure, say so — a dispatcher would far rather have an uncertain report than
no report.

**Do not file false reports.** Knowingly reporting an emergency that is not
happening sends a crew away from someone who needs them. It is also a criminal
offence under Philippine law, and your account will be suspended.

**Keep your details current.** If you move, update your address. Dispatch is
routed from what your account says.

**Look after your account.** Do not share your password. Tell us if you think
someone else has used your account.

**Be at least 18** to hold an account. A parent, guardian, or barangay official
may register on behalf of someone who cannot register themselves.

## 4. Identity verification

We ask for a valid ID and a photograph of your face so an administrator can
confirm that your account belongs to a real person in Biliran.

**You can skip this and still report emergencies.** Verification changes only
what a dispatcher sees about how confident we are in your identity. It is never
a condition of asking for help, and it never delays a report.

Submitting an ID that is not yours, or that has been altered, will result in
your account being suspended.

## 5. Location

Ziren reads your device's location when you submit a report, so a crew can find
you. You can refuse the permission, and the app will ask you to describe where
you are instead — but a described location is slower to reach and easier to get
wrong than a satellite fix.

## 6. Responder accounts

If you register as a responder, your account is inactive until your Agency
Admin has confirmed your badge number and agency ID. You may only use a
responder account for your official duties, and information you see about
residents through it is confidential.

## 7. Availability

We do not promise the service will always be available. It may be down for
maintenance, or because of a fault, a network outage, or a power interruption.
We will keep unplanned downtime as short as we can, but we cannot guarantee
uptime, and this is another reason to know your local hotline number.

## 8. Suspending accounts

We may suspend or remove an account that files false reports, misuses a
responder role, submits a fraudulent ID, or interferes with the operation of
the system. Where circumstances allow, we will tell you why.

## 9. Liability

Ziren is provided as a public service by the participating agencies. To the
extent Philippine law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from a failure
to deliver a report, a delayed or absent response, or your reliance on this app
instead of a direct emergency call.

Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by
negligence, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

## 10. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change is significant, the app will ask you to
read and accept the new version before you continue. You can always read the
current version in Settings.
